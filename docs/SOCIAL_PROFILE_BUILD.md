## Canonical short bio

Track symptoms, medications and lifestyle factors. Understand what changes over time. Prepare for better healthcare conversations. Pamet observes; Pamet does not diagnose.

## Canonical medium bio

Pamet is a privacy-first personal health journal for recording symptoms, medications, mood, activity and lifestyle factors over time, then organizing those observations for personal review and healthcare conversations. Track → Understand → Prepare. Pamet observes; Pamet does not diagnose.

## Brand rules

- Use the existing Pamet app icon / mark as the profile avatar.
- Use `Your health history, finally useful.` as the primary cover/header headline.
- Avoid diagnostic, treatment, emergency-monitoring, HIPAA-compliance, or clinical-outcome claims.
- Do not imply that Pamet replaces professional medical care.
- Do not use advertising-language that suggests Pamet sells or monetizes health data.
- State that Pamet is actively being developed when introducing the product to new audiences.
- Keep health-related UTM parameters generic; never include a symptom, condition, medication or other sensitive user detail in a campaign URL.

## One-time steps that require the account owner

Social platforms commonly require one or more of the following during initial account creation or connection:

- authenticated Google/Meta/platform sign-in;
- email verification;
- SMS/phone verification;
- CAPTCHA or anti-bot challenge;
- acceptance of platform terms;
- confirmation of a business/professional-account conversion;
- selection/authorization of the Pamet Facebook Page or YouTube channel.

These identity and consent checkpoints must be completed by the account owner. After the account exists and is connected to Metricool, routine posting and analytics can be managed from the Pamet Metricool workspace.
