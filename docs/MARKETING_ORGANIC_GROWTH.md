# Pamet Organic Growth Playbook

## Goal

Build a trust-first organic acquisition engine for Pamet that drives qualified visitors to the product without relying on paid advertising.

Primary funnel:

**Helpful content → Pamet marketing landing page → Free account → First health entry → Repeat logging → Visit Brief → Paid upgrade when deeper features become useful**

Pamet should never market itself as diagnosing, treating, monitoring emergencies, replacing a clinician, or producing clinical conclusions.

---

## 1. Facebook Page Setup

### Recommended page identity

- **Page name:** Pamet — Personal Health Journal
- **Preferred username:** `@pamethealth` if available; fallback `@getpamet`
- **Category:** Health/Wellness Website, App Page, or Software as available in Facebook's current category picker
- **Primary CTA:** **Sign Up** if available; otherwise **Learn More**
- **CTA destination after deployment:** `https://pamet.wasmer.app/facebook.html`
- **Profile image:** Pamet app icon / mark
- **Cover direction:** clean Pamet brand background with the headline **Your health history, finally useful.** and subline **Track. Understand. Prepare.**

### Short bio

Privacy-first personal health journal for tracking symptoms, medications and lifestyle over time and preparing clearer healthcare conversations. Pamet observes — it does not diagnose.

### About description

Pamet is a privacy-first personal health journal designed to help you record symptoms, medications, mood, activity and lifestyle factors over time, review changes in your own history, and prepare a concise Visit Brief for healthcare conversations.

Pamet is actively being developed and improved. Some newer features may continue to evolve as feedback is incorporated.

Pamet observes. Pamet does not diagnose. It is not emergency monitoring, a clinical decision tool, or a replacement for professional medical care.

### Recommended pinned post structure

1. The problem: remembering weeks or months of symptoms during a short appointment is difficult.
2. What Pamet does: Track → Understand → Prepare.
3. Privacy/trust statement.
4. Active-development disclosure.
5. One CTA: Try Pamet free.

Do not make every post promotional. The page should feel like a useful health-journaling resource that happens to be built by Pamet.

## 2. Organic Content Strategy

### Content pillars

#### A. Appointment preparation
Examples:
- What to write down before a doctor appointment
- How to summarize a month of changing symptoms
- What information is useful in a symptom journal
- Three things people commonly forget to mention at an appointment

#### B. Symptom-journaling education
Examples:
- What makes a symptom journal useful later
- Why timing and severity matter
- How sleep, activity and medication context can add useful context
- How to log without turning health tracking into homework

#### C. Product demonstrations
Examples:
- 20-second screen recording: log a symptom
- Calendar/history review
- Turning recorded history into a Visit Brief
- Free vs. Pro vs. Ultra: explain use cases rather than hard-selling features

#### D. Founder/build-in-public content
Examples:
- Why Pamet is being built
- What changed this week and why
- How user feedback changed a feature
- Privacy choices Pamet deliberately makes

#### E. Trust and privacy
Examples:
- What local-first means in Pamet
- Pamet contains no advertising on any plan
- What Pamet does not claim or do
- How sharing is designed to be explicit and revocable

## 3. Posting Cadence

Start with a cadence that can be sustained for 8+ weeks:

- **Facebook:** 4 posts/week
- **Facebook Reels:** 2 short videos/week
- **Instagram:** reuse the same 4 posts + 2 Reels
- **TikTok:** reuse 2 short educational/product videos/week
- **YouTube Shorts:** reuse 2 short videos/week
- **Pinterest:** 3 educational pins/week using the same core content
- **Reddit / Facebook Groups:** 2 genuinely useful community contributions/week; promotional links only when community rules allow and only when directly relevant
- **Founder channels (LinkedIn / Indie Hackers):** 1 build-in-public post/week

One piece of content should be repurposed rather than recreated six times.

## 4. First Four Weeks

### Week 1 — Why symptom history matters
- Post: Why it is hard to remember a month of symptoms in a 15-minute appointment
- Reel: Log an entry in Pamet in under 30 seconds
- Educational post: What belongs in a useful symptom journal
- Founder post: Why Pamet exists and what is still being improved

### Week 2 — Appointment preparation
- Post: Five details worth recording before your next appointment
- Reel: From entries to Visit Brief
- Educational post: Symptom severity vs. symptom frequency
- Trust post: Pamet observes — it does not diagnose

### Week 3 — Privacy and simplicity
- Post: Why Pamet has no advertising on any plan
- Reel: Calendar/history walkthrough
- Educational post: How to track health without obsessing over streaks
- Founder post: One user-experience improvement shipped this week

### Week 4 — Product value
- Post: Track → Understand → Prepare explained
- Reel: Free plan walkthrough
- Educational post: How a concise history can make an appointment conversation easier
- Feedback post: Ask users what they wish they remembered at appointments

## 6. Community Outreach Rules

Before posting in a Facebook Group, subreddit, Discord or forum:

1. Read its self-promotion rules.
2. Contribute useful answers before sharing Pamet.
3. Disclose that you are the builder/founder when you mention Pamet.
4. Do not present Pamet as medical advice.
5. Do not target individuals based on sensitive health information with unsolicited marketing.
6. Never ask users to post private health information publicly.

A good community post should still be useful if the Pamet link is removed.

## 7. UTM Convention

Use consistent campaign parameters for every link.

Examples:

- Facebook Page: `?utm_source=facebook&utm_medium=organic_social&utm_campaign=facebook_page`
- Facebook Group: `?utm_source=facebook&utm_medium=community&utm_campaign=community_outreach`
- Instagram: `?utm_source=instagram&utm_medium=organic_social&utm_campaign=organic_launch`
- TikTok: `?utm_source=tiktok&utm_medium=organic_social&utm_campaign=organic_launch`
- YouTube: `?utm_source=youtube&utm_medium=organic_video&utm_campaign=organic_launch`
- Reddit: `?utm_source=reddit&utm_medium=community&utm_campaign=community_outreach`
- Product Hunt: `?utm_source=producthunt&utm_medium=launch&utm_campaign=product_launch`

Do not put health-condition labels, diagnoses, symptoms, medication names, or other sensitive user attributes in URL parameters.

## 8. Metrics That Matter

Do not optimize for follower count alone.

Track:

1. Landing-page visits by source
2. Free-account creation rate
3. First-entry activation rate
4. 7-day returning-user rate
5. Visit Brief creation rate
6. Upgrade conversion by acquisition source
7. Content saves/shares/comments
8. Qualitative feedback themes

A smaller source producing repeat loggers is more valuable than a viral post producing low-intent clicks.

---

## 9. Whop Recommendation

Do **not** make Whop the primary Pamet acquisition or checkout path at this stage.

Reasons:

- Pamet already owns its product/account/subscription experience.
- A second storefront risks fragmenting product identity and user trust.
- Pamet is a privacy-sensitive health-journaling product; the cleanest trust path is directly from Pamet content to the Pamet domain.
- Whop's creator campaign products are better suited to a later paid creator/affiliate experiment than the current free-organic phase.

Potential later use:

- controlled affiliate/creator campaigns after conversion tracking is proven;
- a creator brief for privacy-safe educational UGC;
- never send personal health data to Whop as part of marketing attribution.

---

## 10. Social Scheduling

Recommended operating model:

- Use one social scheduler to connect Facebook + Instagram and optionally TikTok, YouTube, LinkedIn and Pinterest.
- Maintain a four-week rolling content queue.
- Prepare each post once, then adapt only the hook, aspect ratio and CTA per platform.
- Review weekly: top three posts, bottom three posts, traffic created, accounts created, and comments/questions worth turning into future content.

Metricool is a strong fit for this workflow because it can centralize planning, scheduling and performance review across major social channels. Keep the initial setup on the free tier where possible and add paid tooling only after organic traffic proves repeatable.
